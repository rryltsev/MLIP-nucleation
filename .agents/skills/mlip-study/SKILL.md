name: mlip-study
description: Design and continue this project's heterogeneous nucleation study, including candidate selection, hypothesis tests and evidence-to-manuscript traceability.
---

Read `project/brief.txt` and `project/state.json` from the repository root. Preserve the distinction between the proposed interfacial-order mechanism and established evidence.

For system selection, compare chemical coverage of candidate MLIPs, accessible experiments, relevant competing phases, DFT tractability and literature novelty. Al-based alloys with TiB2/TiC/AlB2/Al3Ti are candidates, not an automatic selection. Do not infer novelty from missing search results.

Turn each hypothesis into an observable, intervention, comparison and falsification criterion. For the proposed causal chain, distinguish surface chemistry changes from lattice mismatch, strain, surface termination, roughness, wettability and temperature effects. Predictive correlation alone does not demonstrate mediation by liquid order.

Predefine thermodynamic conditions, phase identification, structural/chemical order descriptors, independent replicas and convergence studies. Separate induction times, nucleation rates and free-energy barriers; do not identify one with another. Validate enhanced-sampling coordinates and sampling convergence before interpreting barriers. Retain censored no-event trajectories and competing phases in the analysis.

Map simulation predictions to a feasible experiment without assuming MD undercooling or timescales directly equal experimental values. Reserve a prospective prediction for a system not used to tune the descriptor.

Record decisions, rejected alternatives and open questions in durable project state. Write reports/manuscripts in LaTeX; each scientific claim must cite verified literature or trace to a result artifact. Report missing evidence as missing, and retain negative results.
